# AI Builder X 🛠️

**The Open Lab for AI Builders**

*Build Intelligence. Engineer Value.*

AI Builder X is a developing open practice community within **[AIVE](https://github.com/AIValueEngineering)**. We explore how people can move from using AI tools to building systems that solve real problems and generate verifiable value.

## Our practice loop

1. **Co-design the blueprint** — clarify the problem, intended value, constraints, responsibilities, and success criteria.
2. **Build and run with AI** — create and test a working agent, skill, workflow, or practical tool.
3. **Evolve through real feedback** — record outcomes, failures, costs, and evidence; then improve, roll back, or stop.

## What belongs here

- Reproducible starter projects and small experiments
- AI skills, agents, workflows, and evaluation practices
- Templates for problem framing, runtime records, and evidence-based decisions
- Open discussions, useful failures, and community contributions

## Project status

**Early-stage project.** Starter kits, contribution guidelines, and real-world challenges are planned. We do not claim verified business results or community adoption before evidence is available.

## Participation and rights

Contributions must respect privacy, intellectual property, and relevant organizational permissions. Do not submit customer data, employer-owned code, secrets, or confidential materials.

A public repository is not automatically open source. Licensing terms for code, documentation, and book-related materials will be defined before accepting substantial external contributions.
